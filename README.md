### **Cookery** – A Culinary App for Sharing and Discovering Recipes  

### **USER FEATURES:**  
#### **REGISTERED USERS CAN:**  
- Publish, edit, and delete their own recipes.  
- Save recipes as drafts.  
- Add recipes to the **"Favorites"** section.  
- Create a **shopping list**.  

#### **UNREGISTERED USERS CAN:**  
- Browse recipes by category.  
- Search for recipes by **ingredients** or **title**.  

---

## **I. LOGIN SCREEN**  
From this screen, the user has three options to continue:  
1. **Log in**  
2. **Sign up**  
3. **Continue without registration**  

---

## **II. REGISTRATION SCREEN**  
To register, a user needs to provide:  
- **Name**  
- **Email**  
- **Password** (entered twice for confirmation)  
- **Profile avatar** selection  

---

## **III. MAIN SCREEN**  
The main screen consists of **four categories**:  
- **Breakfast**  
- **Lunch**  
- **Dinner**  
- **Desserts**  

At the top, there's a **search bar** to easily find desired dishes.  

---

## **IV. SECTIONS**  
Sections are located in the **left-side menu**, along with the main user information.  
The app has **six sections**:  

1. **Home** – The main feed.  
2. **My Recipes** – Recipes created by the user.  
3. **Drafts** – Unfinished recipes.  
4. **Favorites** – Saved recipes (from other users or their own).  
5. **Shopping List** – A list of ingredients the user plans to buy.  
6. **Settings** – Allows the user to:  
   - Change **name, email, password, or profile avatar**.  
   - **Delete** their account along with all their recipes.  

---

## **V. RECIPE SCREEN**  
Opens when a user selects a recipe.  
Includes buttons to:  
- **Copy the recipe**  
- **Add to Favorites**  

---

## **VI. ADDING A RECIPE**  
A screen that opens when the **"+" button** is pressed.  

---

## **VII. EDITING/DELETING A RECIPE**  
To edit or delete a recipe, **long press** on the recipe.  

---

## **FUTURE DEVELOPMENT & ENHANCEMENTS**  
- **Visit other users' profiles** and view their recipes.  
- **Commenting** on recipes.  
- **Adding multiple photos/videos** to a recipe.  
- **Follow other users** and receive notifications about their new recipes.
